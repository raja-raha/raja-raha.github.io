KegaLite Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

KegaLite does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (streak, level, completed sessions) and your reminder time are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Reminders

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push servers are involved.

No tracking

KegaLite has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and it makes no network requests.

Children

KegaLite is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
