Common questions

My reminder doesn't show up. Open Settings → Notifications → KegaLite and make sure notifications are allowed.

Will I lose my streak if I get a new phone? Progress is stored only on your device, so it does not move to a new phone.

Is this medical advice? No. If you have pain, symptoms that worry you, or have had recent surgery, talk to your doctor first.
